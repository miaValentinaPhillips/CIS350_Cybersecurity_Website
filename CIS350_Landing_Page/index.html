Would you trust this email?

Three reasons to pause

  • The sender: an unfamiliar address claiming to be account support.
  • The pressure: a short deadline pushing you to act quickly.
  • The request: an unexpected link asking you to confirm sign-in details.

These clues deserve a check. Open the service's official website yourself and verify the request there.
